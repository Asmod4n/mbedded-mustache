What was measured: the render of the stocks page (workload 2 of bench/render.cpp at 3e49a1c) from std::unordered_map data (run_time: a template compiled at run time)
and from structs (reflection: a template of the source, g++-16 only), per binary, with Google Benchmark 1.9.1, 5 rounds of 6 repetitions,
--benchmark_min_time=0.05s, a new random order of the 14 binaries in each round (seed in context-common.txt), as the user nobody in
localhost/mbedded-cbor-docs. g++-16 -std=c++26 -freflection and clang++-23 -std=c++26, -O2 -march=x86-64-v4 -DNDEBUG and the align flags.
The code of the probe is at commit 1efeb1e8cb776ebada8509c7000e66529dcabec5 (bench/probes/fewer-calls). The base of every arm is include/ of 4e036ed.
Each arm is a patch of that probe directory; head and twin are two copies of the base with no patch. The cells of H5 and H6 on clang++-23 are error cells.
The U test p-values are from tools/compare.py of Google Benchmark 1.9.1 (compare/ARM.COMPILER.txt), arm against head, 30 rows against 30 rows.
